Cluster Annotation Survey

For each cluster you will:

  1. Nominate a pathway and judge the cluster's coherence.
  2. Classify each gene as Established / Novel role / Uncharacterized.

Each gene shows two blinded evidence sources (Source A and Source B) — use either or both, and please web-search (PubMed / Scholar / GeneCards buttons) to ground your call. For each gene we also ask which evidence you preferred and whether you needed web search.

In Stage 1, click any gene to preview its evidence. Progress autosaves in this browser — use Export CSV when done.
